## Wave 3 results (2026-10-02): the College of Education and Human Sciences

**33 programs integrated** in 7 majors (116 programs, 119 plans, 4,823 slots in all): Early Childhood Education (3: Birth-K, PreK-3, Practitioner);
Elementary Education; Exercise Science (11 concentrations, all required: the ten concentration maps and the "Exercise Science" map);
Multidisciplinary Studies (5); Psychology; Secondary Education (9 licensure tracks plus Non-Licensure); Special Education (3). Nothing was held back.

**What it took, and what is in the code now**
- **Adapter, generic:** text that only says another course will do ("EXPW 1021 or equivalent", "or advisor approved A&P") is the course; courses
  listed beside the set they belong to ("MATH 1410 / Quantitative Reasoning") are the set; courses and sets offered together are one choice from
  their union when a pool lists exactly those courses (the tool now writes that pool); several wordings that each name a course are those
  courses; a no-credit course listed every semester (PHED 1002, a fitness test) is planned once; a decision may name a pool for a single course
  the catalog lacks (HEC 4940). 7 tests in `coursedogWave3.test.mjs`.
- **Pools:** 80 new (273 in all): 42 either/or (some are unions with sets), 5 course sets, 27 elective families, plus hand-made (HEC nutrition,
  SPAN upper-division, HIST-or-SPAN upper-division, advisor guided electives, PSY 4990 or 4995).
- **Answers, all `assumed`:** three college-wide waivers for Education (Humanities, literature, social science); HEC 2020 / EXPW 4855 hours; 13 rows
  the map writes inconsistently, mixes with text or offers from courses the catalog lacks; four courses whose prerequisites the maps put in one semester
  (EXPW 4874, 2001, 2220, 4760: the prerequisite became a corequisite) and CSED 3010's corequisite, which the map places a semester later.
- **Hours below 120:** 23 of the 33 plans declare 113-119 hours, because the app counts a course with a credit range at its minimum
  (FOED field experiences, ECED 4261, EXPW 4820: the map counts them at 3, 2 or 7) and counts only the top track of the math placement chain (a
  map that starts at MATH 1710 / 1720 counts less than the department total: Middle School Math is 113). Each spec says so in its assumptions.
  If a student should see 120 required, a fixed ranged course needs a chosen-hours default; that is app work, not data.
- **Verified:** `npm run verify` (1,389 tests, all 116 programs in the conformance suite), Prototype 238 tests, the browser pane (Education tile with
  7 majors, Exercise Science's 11 concentrations, a Fall 2026 Pre-Physical Therapy plan), and the throwaway Postgres (116 programs, 4,823 slots,
  3,549 kept and 0 removed, students untouched, a second run changes nothing). The catalog bundle is 234 KB gzip.
- The program-browser, catalog-years and planner-catalog tests no longer assume a count of programs (the last needs a longer timeout: it builds every
  program for 12 students).

**What it took, and what is in the code now**
- **Pools:** 123 new (193 in all). The tool now writes the pools a wave needs from the snapshot: 42 `OR_` either/or lists, 28 `SET_<set name>` course sets
  (the set's courses the catalog has) and 46 `EL_<wording>` open elective families, and it never changes a pool that exists. Hand-made: Quantitative
  Reasoning (`FF_QUANT`), Foreign Language, Physical Science Elective, the planetary-geology list, the Gulf Coast lab courses, upper-division math (a rule
  pool), the applied-or-pure math sequence union, and four open pools for rows that mix courses with text.
- **Answers recorded** (33 decisions, 2 catalog overrides, all `assumed`): three college-scoped Arts and Sciences waivers (the maps name their own
  Humanities, literature and social-science courses instead of two open rows each), 22 hours disagreements between a map and the catalog (a lecture
  and its lab counted together), the CJ 3610 junior-standing question (59 hours before it on the map), and seven rows the map writes inconsistently
  or mixes with text. Two overrides: PC 2500 loses its ENGL 1020 prerequisite (the Physics maps put it in semester 1) and CJ 3610 its standing.
  `PC2500` was a curated course, so the curated-requisites fixture and the parser test's known divergences changed with it.
- **Code:** a map with year-only columns or a co-op block has no recommended order (**6 plans are unscheduled**: Industrial Chemistry and five
  Mathematics plans; the builder places them); a bundle offered as alternatives is the union of its courses (k slots when every bundle has k);
  a set the vocabulary says *is* the courses (`expand: all` / `sequence`: Communication - Writing, Historical Foundations); two sets offered as
  alternatives take a union pool; hours written as ranges ("2-3 credits") are ignored when matching wordings; a course written as text
  ("POLS1001 (1 cr.)") is that course; a text row with 0 hours is a note; one wording offered twice is one wording; a row of m x the pool's hours
  is m picks; a `coursedog-row` decision may name the pool of a courses row. **`plannerCodes` now loads only the pools the plan has**: it loaded every
  pool of every program, which would have passed the Docker API's 1000-row cap as waves 3-5 arrive (the slice was 568 courses for CSC at this point).
- **Math curriculum of an unscheduled plan:** `mathCurriculumOf` read "this plan requires MATH 1920" from the map's semester for it, so the five
  unscheduled Mathematics plans were read as the new curriculum and declared 116 hours. A Flight Foundations plan with no map that has a MATH 1920
  slot is now `returning` in the validator and in promotion. The app's `mathCurriculumFor` still decides from `map_semester` (its slot queries do not
  select `gened_program`): for those plans a freshman gets the new chain with MATH 1920 as an ordinary slot, so the two agree on hours, not on the
  label. Revisit if a plan ever needs the placement chain to include MATH 1920.
- **Earlier entrants:** as in wave 1, every first plan covers earlier years, flagged as an approximate fit.
- **Verified:** `npm run verify` (1,257 tests; the all-programs conformance suite covers all 83), Prototype 231 tests, the browser pane (the picker's
  college tiles with Arts & Sciences's 14 majors, the approximate-fit note for a Fall 2023 Mathematics entrant, a 120-hour plan whose issues are the
  heavy-load warning and unfilled choices), and a **real Postgres**: the throwaway `mdp_fftest` stack (wave 1's 36 programs, 1,759 slots, 2 students,

  stack's `.env` and the auth container fails on the password.
- **For the college to confirm:** the three waivers; that elective families are open choices (the restrictions in a row's wording are not enforced); the
  22 hours disagreements; PC 2500's prerequisite and CJ 3610's standing; the Foreign Language or Math rows (History B.S.); that Theatre and PTC wait for

## Goal

A student picks **college, major, concentration** first, then their start term, and gets the department's own degree map
as a plan, for every Tennessee Tech bachelor's program that publishes one. The four CSC-department maps and the five
engineering maps proved the pipeline (`degree-specs/`, extractor, pools as data, map-first builder). This plan scales it
from 9 programs to 175 and reorders onboarding to match.

### 1.3 How good is the Coursedog data

**Parity against the department maps we already integrated.** For the 9 programs (CSC Core, Cybersecurity, HPC, AI, ME x4,

either/or rows, course sets and free-text rows.

**Shape of the 175 maps (7,175 rows):** 4,085 single-course rows (60 of them AND bundles: a list of codes such as
lecture + lab or nursing theory + clinical), 560 either/or course rows, 1,223 course-set rows, 1,252 free-text rows, 55
rows mixing types.

- Course coverage is nearly complete: **2 codes** are missing from our catalog (HEC 1030, HEC 4940, both inactive; the build
  already keeps an inactive course a spec names). Some rows name a course by its internal group id, resolvable through
  `catalog_raw.json` (one resolves to an inactive `ME3320 ... DUPLICATE` record, so resolve to the canonical code).
- **80 distinct course sets**; six gen-ed sets account for 990 of the 1,223 uses (Humanities and/or Fine Arts,
  Social/Behavioral, Natural Sciences, Mathematics, and two more). Sets are fetchable by id; 2 of the 6 big ones returned 404

- Free text is repetitive: 523 distinct wordings after numbers are normalized; the top ones are "Electives (N cr.)",
  "Elective (N cr.)", "Major ensemble", "Applied music", "Foreign language".
- Resolved hours (from our catalog; rows with no stated credits count 0): 152 programs land at 115-140 h, 22 at 100-114
  (a data gap in the rows, not a short degree), 1 outlier (Accelerated Nursing, 63 h, 9 summer-to-summer terms).
- **Shapes the pipeline does not handle yet:** 5 maps (all Math concentrations) are 4 columns labelled by year only; RN-BSN

  Accelerated Nursing on a summer-to-summer calendar); two programs have
  **two tracks** (International Commerce and Cultures; Music Live Audio Engineering Technology); Professional Studies
  Health Administration appears twice under one name; `isActive` on a map is unreliable (many shown maps say false: use
  `displayedInCatalog`).
- Typing quirks: "Mathematics, B.S." is typed a concentration but is the major's base map; "Civil Engineering, Geological
  Engineering Concentration" is typed a major. Coursedog has no parent link (`parentName` is empty on all 175), so the

## 3. Plan: integrate them all

### 3.1 Data model (flat, no new tables)

- `programs.json` gains `college` (a code), `majorCode` (stable group key: two majors can share a name, such as History
  B.A. and B.S. or Music B.M. and B.S., so the key is college + major + degree), `isBase` (the "no concentration" choice),
  `concentrationRequired`, `coursedogId` (so a re-pull maps back), `track`, optional `aliases` ("CS", "cyber").
- `colleges.json`: code, display name, order, URL.
- `concentrations` table gets `college`, `major_code`, `is_base` through `000_baseline.sql` (`ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`), emitted by
  `build:catalog` and `seed.js`. The frontend already reads optional program columns tolerantly (`selectWithOptional`).
  **No student table changes, so no `STUDENT_TABLES` default; no migration file.**
- Stored profiles keep `concentration_id` and `catalog_year`; nothing about an existing student changes.

### 3.2 Pools and free text

80 course sets become `pools.json` entries (stable code, the Coursedog group id as provenance; a pool is not scoped to a
catalog year, so a changed list gets a new code, per the existing rule). Free text is classified vocabulary-first; the target is
that nearly all of the ~1,250 rows classify automatically, with the remainder listed per department. Fine Arts (ensembles,
applied lessons) and Education (licensure rows) will need the most vocabulary.

### 3.3 Code that must generalize (found by reading, to be proven by the conformance suite)

1. AND bundles (lecture + lab, theory + clinical) as paired slots.
2. Year-only and irregular-calendar maps (map semester null plus a visible "no recommended order" note).
3. Two-track programs as separate programs sharing a major.
4. Math placement: the placement chain and `mathCurriculumFor` were built around calculus majors. Majors whose map has no
   calculus (Music, Nursing, many Education programs) must not gain math slots; the earlier Nuclear Engineering bug (a MATH 2010
   slot the map does not have) is the same class.
5. Anything else that assumes CSC or engineering (ACT-gated course text, `semesterRestrictions`).

**Safety net, built first:** a data-driven **conformance suite** over every promoted program: a standard Fall-start student with no
prior credit must get the department map back, semester by semester, with matching totals. A program that fails is listed by
name instead of shipping wrong.

### 3.4 Size

`requirement_slots` is 594 rows today (135 KB raw, 6 KB gzip, because slots repeat). 175 programs is roughly 7,300 slots: about
1.7 MB raw and about 75 KB gzip (an estimate from today's ratio). `catalog.json` goes from 133 KB to about 210 KB gzip; the course
table is still the bulk. Slots are read per program and year, so the Docker API's 1,000-row cap is not hit; `concentrations`
and `degree_plans` stay under 1,000 rows. Rule: measure in Wave 0; if `catalog.json` passes about 300 KB gzip, lazy-load plans per college
the way descriptions already load.

## 4. Plan: onboarding

### 4.1 Today

Step 1 is student type plus start season and year; step 2 is a flat list of programs grouped one level by `major_name`,
filtered by the entry year (`availablePrograms(entryYear)`); steps 3-5 are scores, math sequence, prior credits. The year lists
are hard-wired to the 2026 switch (`RETURNING_YEARS`, `NEW_STUDENT_YEARS`). A flat list of 175 programs does not work.

### 4.2 New flow

1. **What are you studying?** One step with progressive disclosure: **college** (8 tiles, or the search box which skips ahead)
   → **major** (degree title, department, "n concentrations") → **concentration**. A major with a base map offers it as the
   first choice ("no concentration"); a concentration-required major does not. Selection shows as a breadcrumb
   (Engineering › Mechanical Engineering › Aerospace) with Change. Search matches name, major, department, aliases, like
   tntech.edu/majors. Programs closed to new students (DSAI) are hidden behind "looking for a program that is no longer offered?".
2. **When do you start?** The current step 1 content (student type, season, year), now **constrained by the program chosen**:
   the offered years are those where `planForYear` finds a plan for that program. If the chosen major has no plan for that
   year, say so in place and offer: another year, another major (Back), or, for a closed program, its replacement
   (`supersedes`: DSAI → AI) in one click. The 2026-hard-wired year lists become data-driven.
3. **Test scores** stays for everyone (ACT English and SAT/ACT Math also drive prior credit).
4. **Math sequence** is shown only when the plan has a math placement chain (resolved from the plan's slots, which step 3 already
   loads); otherwise the flow skips it and the progress bar has four steps.
5. **Prior credits** unchanged; the import-a-backup link stays on the first screen.

Settings "change program" renders its own copy of today's flat picker (`SettingsView.jsx`, same `availablePrograms` and
`groupByMajor` calls), so the new picker becomes one shared component used by both screens. New pure helpers in `catalogYears.js`: `groupByCollege`, `searchPrograms`,
`programYears`, each with tests. The verified browser-driving snippet in `docs/claude/CLAUDE.md` ("Driving onboarding in the browser
pane") will no longer match the screens; updating it is part of the same change.

Mobile: a single column, search first, college as an accordion. Keyboard and screen-reader behavior: radio groups and a proper
listbox for search results.

### 4.3 Out of scope here

Undecided / exploratory majors, minors, certificates, double majors, graduate programs.
